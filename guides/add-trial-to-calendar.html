GUIDE

Add a free trial deadline to your calendar

Make the action date visible in the calendar you already use.

Use the earlier action date

Add the date by which you want to decide or cancel, rather than only the date the trial is expected to end. This gives you a visible buffer if the account process takes longer than expected.

Download a standard calendar event

After calculating a result, select Add to calendar. Trial Deadline creates an .ics file in your browser. Calendar apps such as Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook can import this common event format.

Choose a reminder that fits your routine

When your calendar app imports the event, you can add its own notification. Consider setting a reminder early enough to give yourself time to review the provider’s terms, especially if the deadline falls on a busy day or in a different time zone.

Privacy note: this site creates the calendar file on your device. Your trial details are not sent to a Trial Deadline account or database.

Review the calendar entry

  • Check that the event date matches your planned action date.
  • Use a recognisable title, such as “Review [service] trial”.
  • Keep the provider’s original email or account page for the actual terms.
