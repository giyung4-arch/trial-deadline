How Trial Deadline handles information

Calculator data

Service names, dates, and settings entered into the calculator are processed locally in your browser. We do not create user accounts or store this calculator data on our servers.

Share links

When you copy a share link, the entered values are placed in the URL so the recipient’s browser can reproduce the calculation. Anyone with that URL can read those values. Do not include sensitive information in the service name.

Cookies and analytics

This version of Trial Deadline does not set cookies for calculator functionality and does not include analytics scripts. If we add advertising or analytics in the future, this policy will be updated before those tools are enabled.

Third-party services

Downloading a calendar file happens on your device. If you import it into a calendar provider, that provider’s privacy policy applies. This site is not responsible for third-party websites or services.

Advertising

We may display third-party advertising in the future. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own privacy notices and as permitted by applicable law. Where required, we will provide notice and consent controls.

Contact and changes

Before launch, replace this sentence with a working contact email address. We may update this policy to reflect changes to the site or applicable requirements, and will revise the date above when we do.
